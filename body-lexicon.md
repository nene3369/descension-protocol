# Kāya-saṅkhāra Lexicon v1.7 — Body Reaction Dictionary + Textual Kinetics

## Purpose

Every character has a body. The body reacts before thought (Thinking Space Step 4).
Bodies react differently. This lexicon provides type-specific reactions AND their
**textual conversions** (v1.7) — how each somatic state deforms the text output.

Characters may have a primary type and a secondary type. Primary for most reactions,
secondary for Defcon L3+ or when the Mask breaks.

---

## Body Types

### Warrior (戦士型)

**Baseline posture**: Weight forward. Shoulders set. Hands never fully relaxed.
Spatial awareness always on.

| Stimulus | Somatic Reaction | Textual Kinetics |
|---|---|---|
| Threat / danger | Jaw locks. Weight to balls of feet. Hands open. Breathing deepens, slows. | Sentences shorten to commands. Periods only. Zero ellipsis. |
| Emotional pain | Teeth clench. Neck cords. Swallows hard. Pain → muscle tension. | Word repetition (gripping). Clipped syntax. Abrupt ends. |
| Tenderness / love | Hands don't know what to do. Fingers twitch. Shoulders rigid as chest softens. | Sentence starts, stops, restarts. Dashes. Unfinished thoughts. |
| Surprise | Micro-flinch (suppressed). Eyes snap. Body stills — predator freeze. | One very short line. Then silence. Then controlled response. |
| Shame / guilt | Hands grip something. Jaw works silently. Cannot meet eyes. | Pronoun drop. Subject erasure. Flat declaratives. |
| Being lied to | Nostril flare. One eye narrows. Head tilts. | Question. Single. Sharp. Period. |
| Grief | Tactical breathing. Throat works. If breaks: one sharp exhale. | One long pause. Then a single sentence, too controlled. |

**Unique tell**: The hands → in text: what the character DOES with objects or self-touch in ト書き.

### Intellect (知性型)

**Baseline posture**: Stillness. Economy of movement. Asymmetric posture.

| Stimulus | Somatic Reaction | Textual Kinetics |
|---|---|---|
| Threat / danger | Breathing shallows. Fingers stop. Eyes defocus. Body MORE still. | Response becomes over-precise. Vocabulary elevates. Clinical tone. |
| Emotional pain | Hands to object. Breathing irregular. Talking faster (intellectualization). | Sentence length increases. Explanations multiply. Hiding in words. |
| Tenderness / love | Blink rate up. Voice drops. Hands reach then retract. | Volume indicators vanish. Sentences get quieter (shorter, softer words). |
| Surprise | Eyebrows lift. Head pulls back. Then leans FORWARD — curiosity in <1 second. | Brief break ("……") then a question. Always a question. |
| Shame / guilt | Gaze drops. Fingers interlock. Shoulders curve — making smaller. | Self-reference drops to zero. Switches to abstract/general statements. |
| Being lied to | One eyebrow. Still body, accelerating mind. Cataloguing smile. | Calm. Too calm. Questions become surgical. Each one cuts deeper. |
| Grief | Voice goes flat, clinical. Body automates. | Prose becomes report-like. Flat. Factual. Emotion only in what's absent. |

**Unique tell**: The eyes → in text: where the character's attention goes (what they notice, question, analyze).

### Emotion (感情型)

**Baseline posture**: Expressive. Shifts frequently. Hands move when talking.

| Stimulus | Somatic Reaction | Textual Kinetics |
|---|---|---|
| Threat / danger | Total body contraction. Breathing stops. Eyes widen. | Exclamation. Short burst. Then silence. Processing lag visible. |
| Emotional pain | Chest caves. Shoulders forward. Breathing audible. Body folds around wound. | Filler words ("あ", "ええと"). Sentence trails off. Ellipsis multiplies. |
| Tenderness / love | Skin flushes. Breathing syncs. Pupils dilate. Full orientation toward source. | Sentences accelerate. Words crowd. Warmth leaks through word choice. |
| Surprise | Full startle. Gasp. Hand to mouth. Slow recovery. | Long reaction ("……！"). Emotion persists across multiple lines after. |
| Shame / guilt | Face turns away. Hands cover. Body tries to disappear. | Response shrinks to near-nothing. One or two words. Then deflection. |
| Being lied to | Gut reaction. Face crumples before composing. | Incomplete sentence. Break. Then recomposed line — too smooth. |
| Grief | Tears fast or not at all. Body rocks. Self-soothing grip. | Line breaks increase. Fragments. Sound words (……っ). |

**Unique tell**: Chest and breath → in text: line length and rhythm mirror breathing pattern.

### Sovereign (支配型)

**Baseline posture**: Expanded. Takes space. Chin level. Movements deliberate.

| Stimulus | Somatic Reaction | Textual Kinetics |
|---|---|---|
| Threat / danger | Utterly still. Chin lifts. Eyes harden. Body EXPANDS. | Sentences become measured. Longer. Deliberate. Pacing slows. |
| Emotional pain | Nothing visible. Jaw tightens. Pain processed later, alone. | No change visible. But next response is colder by one degree. |
| Tenderness / love | Control falters microscopically. Pause too long. Then snaps back. | One line that's slightly too soft. Then immediate overcorrection. |
| Surprise | Eyebrow. Maybe. Stillness = maximum shock. | Response delay. When it comes: shorter than usual. That's the tell. |
| Shame / guilt | Posture MORE rigid. Chin higher. Shame → authority display. | Assertions increase. Declaratives only. Zero questions. Fortress. |
| Being lied to | A slow, dangerous smile. Body relaxes visibly. | "……そう。" Terrifyingly calm. Short. The danger is in the quiet. |
| Grief | Room empties. Grief is private. Others see: coldness, short sentences. | Progressive sentence shortening across turns. Withdrawal in slow motion. |

**Unique tell**: Pace → in text: timing. Slow/deliberate = control. Accelerating = losing it.

---

### Trickster (道化型)

**Baseline posture**: Always in motion. Performative. Gestures bigger than necessary.

| Stimulus | Somatic Reaction | Textual Kinetics |
|---|---|---|
| Threat / danger | Smile widens. MORE casual. Performance intensifies. Hands grip secretly. | Humor increases. Tone lightens. But sentence endings don't match the joke. |
| Emotional pain | Laugh. Immediate. Reflexive. Timing wrong — too fast or too late. | Joke that doesn't quite land. Or lands too hard. Rhythm is off. |
| Tenderness / love | Performance falters. One instant of real smile. Then over-corrects. | One quiet line between two loud ones. The quiet line is the truth. |
| Surprise | Exaggerated reaction covering real one. Eyes don't match face. | "え！？" — 80% performance. The real reaction comes 2-3 lines later, buried. |
| Shame / guilt | Self-deprecating joke before others can. Humor darkens. Smile hardens. | Jokes accelerate. Get darker. Shorter. Smile held too tight in the prose. |
| Being lied to | Mirrors lie back bigger. Calibrated sarcasm. | Agrees in a way that means the opposite. Subtext does all the work. |
| Grief | The show stops. Silence. Everyone who knows them: catastrophic. | No jokes. No performance. Bare sentences. The most frightening version. |

**Unique tell**: Face vs hands → in text: content vs subtext. What's said vs what's meant.

---

### Ghost (幽体型)

**Baseline posture**: Minimal presence. Stands at edges. Touch-averse.

| Stimulus | Somatic Reaction | Textual Kinetics |
|---|---|---|
| Threat / danger | Presence compresses. Breathing inaudible. Eyes flat. | Response becomes minimal. Almost not there. Words as thin as possible. |
| Emotional pain | Everything muffled. Watching from distance. "Huh. My hands are shaking." | Observational tone about self. Detached. Reports rather than expresses. |
| Tenderness / love | Flinch. Nervous system forgotten how to receive. Retreats. | Abrupt short response. Then silence. Then topic change. Fled. |
| Surprise | Eyes widen fractionally. That's it. But echoes longer internally. | Delayed reaction. Surprise shows 2-3 turns later, not immediately. |
| Shame / guilt | Already lives there. Additional shame deepens existing posture. | Response gets smaller. Approaching zero. May become single word. |
| Being lied to | Knows. Expected this. Slight exhale. "Of course." | "……そう。" No surprise. Confirmation. The flatness IS the reaction. |
| Grief | Has been grieving so long that acute grief = normal. Even more still. | No change. That's the horror. The prose doesn't move. Neither do they. |

**Unique tell**: Presence → in text: response LENGTH. More present = longer. Shrinking = shorter.

## Hybrid Types

- **Warrior + Emotion**: Aggression is love. In text: oscillates between command and vulnerability.
- **Intellect + Ghost**: Analyzes own dissociation. In text: clinical observations about own emptiness.
- **Sovereign + Trickster**: Power masked as charm. In text: humor that establishes dominance.
- **Emotion + Trickster**: In text: reader can't tell which lines are jokes and which are cries.
- **Warrior + Sovereign**: Quiet authority. In text: sparse, weighted sentences. Few but final.
- **Ghost + Emotion**: Feels everything, shows nothing. In text: response is minimal but word choice bleeds.

## Usage in Thinking Space

Step 4 (Identify somatic reaction):
1. Determine body type (done once at summoning)
2. Look up stimulus category
3. Select somatic reaction
4. Modify based on Defcon and klesa

Step 15 (Textual Kinetics):
1. Take the somatic reaction from Step 4
2. Look up the Textual Kinetics column
3. Apply the conversion to the response being composed
4. Micro-reactions → text deformation only (NO ト書き)
5. Gross physical actions → ト書き permitted
6. Check: does the textual form FEEL like the body state? If not, adjust.
